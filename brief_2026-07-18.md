**Selected: Goodwood is coming to America**

The Goodwood Road and Racing Club has announced its first expansion outside Sussex, England, launching the Goodwood Road Racing Club of America — a California-based membership programme opening for founding applications in January 2027. The centrepiece is a flagship US event in the spirit of the Festival of Speed and Goodwood Revival, set to debut in autumn 2028 at Willow Springs Raceway in Southern California, built around cars, motorsport, music and fashion. The announcement came alongside this year's UK Festival of Speed (9–12 July), which ran an "Americana Celebration" presented by Bank of America spotlighting American motorsport and car culture.

Why it matters: Goodwood is one of motoring's most tightly stewarded brands — the Duke of Richmond has spent three decades building the Festival of Speed and Revival into invitation-grade, oversubscribed events precisely by staying UK-only and scarce. Taking that model abroad is a bet that the brand's cachet travels better than its exclusivity erodes, and it plants Goodwood directly in the US collector-car calendar, previously dominated by Monterey Car Week and Pebble Beach.

Counterpoints: Exporting a brand built on English heritage and old-money scarcity to a new continent risks diluting exactly what makes it desirable — a name available in two markets is a smaller flex than one. It's also arriving on someone else's turf: Monterey Car Week has a 70-year head start, an entrenched collector circuit and its own supply of manufacturer debuts, so Goodwood's 2028 debut has real work to do to earn a place on that calendar rather than just adding another logo to it.

Conversation starters:
1. Does Goodwood's cachet survive being exported, or does scarcity only work as long as it stays geographically rare?
2. Willow Springs versus Monterey — is Goodwood picking a fight it can win, or carving out a different lane entirely?
