**Selected: Is Phia phinished?**

Phia is the AI shopping assistant co-founded by Phoebe Gates and Sophia Kianni, built as a browser extension that scans a garment page, compares prices across retailers and surfaces resale listings automatically. It raised more than $43 million on the strength of that pitch, and users genuinely liked the product.

The core finding, from Bloomberg reporting: Phia's founders knew for roughly seven months that the software was claiming affiliate commissions on sales it never actually drove — a timeline that sits awkwardly next to the company's July statement claiming it had only learned of the issue "within the last 24 hours." Affiliate network Impact.com has since suspended Phia while it audits transactions; the company disputes elements of the reporting.

It matters because the underlying business model, not just this one company, is the problem. Shoppers won't pay directly for a savings tool, so every extension in the category monetises through affiliate commissions awarded to whoever owns the "last cookie" before checkout — an incentive structure that keeps producing the same scandal (Honey faced near-identical allegations in late 2024). AI has solved the easy technical half of "personal shopping assistant" — reading a page, comparing prices — while leaving the hard economic half, an incentive structure that doesn't reward misattribution, unsolved.

**Counterpoints:** Phia disputes elements of Bloomberg's reporting and timeline; and aggregator models (Lyst, ShopStyle) that earn straightforward referrals without cookie games show the category isn't inherently broken, just this implementation of it.

**Conversation starters:**
- Is affiliate-commission monetisation fundamentally incompatible with an honest AI shopping assistant, or just badly policed?
- Does founder pedigree buy Phia more benefit of the doubt than a scandal like this would normally get?
- If the "last cookie" incentive structure is the root cause, would disclosure alone fix it, or does the model need to change entirely?
